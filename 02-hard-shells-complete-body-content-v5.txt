<!-- 7. HOW TO CHOOSE BUYER'S GUIDE (#how-to-choose) -->
<div id="how-to-choose" style="margin-top: 40px; margin-bottom: 40px; background-color: #121212; border: 1px solid #222222; border-radius: 8px; padding: 24px; box-sizing: border-box; font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif;">
  
  <div style="margin-bottom: 20px; border-left: 4px solid #ff4d00; padding-left: 12px;">
    <h2 style="color: #ffffff; font-size: 1.6rem; font-weight: 900; text-transform: uppercase; margin: 0 0 4px 0;">How to Choose a Backcountry Hard Shell</h2>
    <p style="color: #aaaaaa; font-size: 0.9rem; margin: 0;">A buyer's guide to waterproof membranes, fabric denier, and touring ventilation.</p>
  </div>

  <div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(280px, 1fr)); gap: 20px; color: #cccccc; font-size: 0.88rem; line-height: 1.5;">
    
    <!-- Guide Box 1 -->
    <div style="background-color: #161616; border: 1px solid #222222; border-radius: 6px; padding: 18px;">
      <h3 style="color: #ff4d00; font-size: 1.05rem; font-weight: 800; text-transform: uppercase; margin: 0 0 8px 0;">1. Hard Shell vs. Soft Shell</h3>
      <p style="margin: 0;">
        <strong>Hard Shells (3-Layer)</strong> utilize a waterproof/breathable membrane sandwiched between a durable face fabric and protective inner lining. They are mandatory for coastal BC storms, heavy snowfall, and wet wind protection in Rogers Pass. <strong>Soft Shells</strong> offer superior breathability and stretch for dry, cold interior climates but lack full stormproof weatherproofing.
      </p>
    </div>

    <!-- Guide Box 2 -->
    <div style="background-color: #161616; border: 1px solid #222222; border-radius: 6px; padding: 18px;">
      <h3 style="color: #ff4d00; font-size: 1.05rem; font-weight: 800; text-transform: uppercase; margin: 0 0 8px 0;">2. Membrane Technologies Compared</h3>
      <p style="margin: 0;">
        • <strong>Gore-Tex Pro (ePE):</strong> Maximum durability and stormproofing with eco-friendly PFAS-free membrane technology.<br>
        • <strong>Gore-Tex Active:</strong> Thinner, highly air-permeable membrane engineered for maximum aerobic breathability.<br>
        • <strong>Dermizax NX:</strong> Non-porous membrane offering exceptional dynamic stretch and zero pore-clogging over time.
      </p>
    </div>

    <!-- Guide Box 3 -->
    <div style="background-color: #161616; border: 1px solid #222222; border-radius: 6px; padding: 18px;">
      <h3 style="color: #ff4d00; font-size: 1.05rem; font-weight: 800; text-transform: uppercase; margin: 0 0 8px 0;">3. Understanding Fabric Denier (D)</h3>
      <p style="margin: 0;">
        Denier measures the thread thickness of the outer face fabric. <strong>30D–40D fabrics</strong> (like the Norrøna Lyngen) are ultralight and pack small for technical touring. <strong>75D–80D fabrics</strong> (like the Arc'teryx Rush or MH Boundary Ridge) provide bombproof resistance against sharp tree branches, ski edges, and heavy pack straps.
      </p>
    </div>

    <!-- Guide Box 4 -->
    <div style="background-color: #161616; border: 1px solid #222222; border-radius: 6px; padding: 18px;">
      <h3 style="color: #ff4d00; font-size: 1.05rem; font-weight: 800; text-transform: uppercase; margin: 0 0 8px 0;">4. Touring Ventilation &amp; Fit</h3>
      <p style="margin: 0;">
        When skinning uphill, pit zips and dual-front zippers allow instant heat dumping without removing your pack. Ensure your shell features an <strong>articulated athletic cut</strong> that accommodates a mid-layer puffy without binding at the shoulders during kick turns or bootpacking.
      </p>
    </div>
